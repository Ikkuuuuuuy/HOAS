### Applied Flowchart Standards & Rules:
1. **Clean Merging Line Rule**:
   - The 4 vertical column lines drop down to meet the bottom horizontal collector bar as **plain solid lines (no arrowheads pointing at the collector bar)**.
   - A **single downward arrow** comes out from the center of the collector line pointing into **`Logout`**, and then into **`END`**.
2. **Explicit 'Yes' & 'No' Labels**:
   - Every decision diamond branch is explicitly labeled **"Yes"** (green) and **"No"** (red).
3. **Single Start & Single End**:
   - Exactly **1 `START`** oval at top center.
   - Exactly **1 `Logout`** process and **1 `END`** oval at bottom center.
4. **Standard Symbols**:
   - ⭕ **Oval**: `START` / `END`
   - ▱ **Parallelogram**: Inputs & Outputs
   - ▭ **Rectangle**: Processes
   - ♢ **Diamond**: Binary Decision points (`Yes` / `No`)
